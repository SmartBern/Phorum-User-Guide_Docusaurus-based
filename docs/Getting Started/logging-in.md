# Logging in

1. From the homepage, click **Login**.

    ![Phorum login page link](/img/screenshots/register_on_phorum.png)

    This will take you to the login page.

    ![Phorum login form](/img/screenshots/login_form.png)

2. Enter a username.

3. Enter a password.

4. Click the **Login** button to log in to your account.

5. After a successful login, you should see your **Profile** button in the navbar on desktop display and in the nav menu on mobile display.

    ![Profile button in desktop navbar](/img/screenshots/profile_button.png)

    ![Profile link in mobile nav menu](/img/screenshots/profile_button_mobile.png)
